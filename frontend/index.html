FAQ

About the faucet

Answers about the free faucet, claim limits, larger developer amounts and Sepolia testnet ETH.

Is the faucet really free?

Yes. The faucet sends 0.02 Sepolia ETH without a payment or wallet connection. Claims are limited to once every 24 hours and are subject to abuse-prevention checks.

Why is there also a paid option?

The paid delivery option is intended for developers who need substantially more Sepolia ETH than a small faucet claim provides. Payment is in native USDC on Arbitrum One and is separate from the free faucet.

Can I send Sepolia ETH to another wallet?

Yes. Enter any valid EVM address that you control or intend to fund on Ethereum Sepolia.

Can Sepolia ETH be converted to mainnet ETH?

No. Sepolia ETH is native to the Sepolia test network and cannot be redeemed for Ethereum mainnet ETH.

Privacy

Privacy notice

The service processes the information required to create and fulfil an order, including recipient wallet addresses, order amount and order status. If you proceed to payment, the payment wallet address and submitted transaction hash are also processed for on-chain verification.

Blockchain transactions are public by design. When blockchain RPC providers are used to verify balances, payments or payouts, those providers may receive technical request data. The web server may also process standard connection data such as IP addresses, timestamps and request paths for operation and security. For free-faucet abuse prevention, the service stores a keyed one-way pseudonymous identifier derived from the requesting IP address rather than storing that address in the faucet-claim record.

We currently do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics on this page. Order and technical data are retained only as needed for service operation, troubleshooting, abuse prevention and applicable legal obligations.

Privacy questions can be sent to getsepolia@protonmail.de.
